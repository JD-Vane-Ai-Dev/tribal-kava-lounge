# Tribal Kava Lounge — V2 Concept (2026-11-10)
Status: initial black/brass concept superseded by owner feedback; two botanical kava-bar concepts are current previews.

## Superseded first direction: Neighborhood After Dark / editorial social lounge
- **Thesis:** A physical third place, not a generic bar, captured through owned photography, condensed headline typography, dark ink surfaces and warm brass highlights.
- **Visual DNA:** real unretouched on-site photography; type-led editorial hierarchy; black/ink + brass; asymmetrical sections; minimal movement; plain functional CTAs.
- **Color:** ink #0a0b0b; ink2 #121413; cream #f5eee1; brass #debc7d; mint #b4cfbd; muted #c2c0b8.
- **Typography:** Barlow Condensed display (OFL, Google Fonts), DM Sans body (OFL), local sans fallbacks; no license-restricted templates.
- **Audience intents:** find location, decide what to drink, see today's activities, inquire about private event rental.
- **CTA hierarchy:** menu first for exploring, directions for an immediate visit, dedicated venue quote for rentals. Working links reuse production URLs.
- **Constraints:** no fake reviews or opening hours, no stock interiors, preserve existing site routes, Netlify Forms and existing SEO content. Introductory rental price only for select off-peak dates.
- **Accessibility:** semantic header/main/sections, one h1, visible focus, contrast-safe body copy, responsive at 320–1440, reduced-motion override, 44px+ control target.
- **Preview:** Never auto-promote to primary homepage without user review; preview remains noindex.
- **QA:** visit/menu/events/venue links, phone and map; responsive no horizontal overflow; screenshot after deploy; no live Form changes.
## Alternative Design DNA directions
1. **Community First:** warm off-white background and neighborhood message-board style; strong readability but loses evening atmosphere.
2. **After Hours Editorial (rejected by owner):** ink/brass with owner photos; coherent with Tribal's real bar and late-night audience.
3. **Electric Game Lounge:** high-contrast neon gaming motif; differentiates gaming but risks underselling corporate rentals and overwhelming mobile visitors.

## Owner revision (Nov 10, 2026): Kava bar, not venue rental
Owner rejected the former black/brass styling as too banquet/lounge-like and directed removal of private-rental promotion from the home page. The rental landing page remains intact and reachable by direct URL; site-wide public promotion is deliberately omitted from the concept home. Regular community events remain as kava bar programming, not rental offers.

Two reviewable directions:
- Botanical: deep forest green, leaf/lime, natural cream, fruit-warm accent; more daytime and first-time-friendly.
- Night Garden: botanical plum, hibiscus pink, light sage; stronger evening identity while retaining a clear kava-specific message.
Both use only repository-owned photos and a CSS illustration of a kava bowl (not presented as a product photo). The hero now leads with kava and community, not rental prices.
