Yes. Your ticket is flexible across 7–10 PM or 10:30 PM–1:30 AM on Friday night, October 30, into Saturday morning. It covers one walkthrough total. No entry during the 10–10:30 PM break.

No. Your bundle includes a shirt and one admission. You do not have to wear the shirt on event night.
