# Kratom Tea at a Kava Bar: Understanding the Menu Before You Order

Kratom tea can appear on a kava bar menu because the lounge serves more than one kind of botanical beverage. At Tribal Kava Lounge in West Palm Beach, kava and kratom have separate menu categories, alongside fruit drinks without either botanical. The name of the venue tells you where you are meeting; the description of the individual drink tells you what you are ordering.

That distinction matters when you are new to the menu. A friend might say, “We are going for kava,” while the people at the table choose several different drinks. Ask about your own cup rather than assuming everybody is ordering the same ingredient. Tribal's kratom products are for adults 21+ only, with valid ID required.

## Kava and kratom are different ingredients

Kava is made from the root of the kava plant. Kratom beverages are made from the leaves of Mitragyna speciosa. They are separate botanical ingredients, even when both appear behind the same counter. A crafted drink can also look different from a straightforward tea without becoming a different plant.

Use our [kava-versus-kratom guide](https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/kava-vs-kratom) for the basic distinction, then ask the team which base is in the menu item you are considering. If somebody says “botanical tea,” it is reasonable to ask which botanical they mean. A clear answer is more helpful than trying to decode the name from the color of the drink.

## What does brewed kratom tea mean on Tribal's menu?

Tribal lists brewed kratom tea separately from crafted kratom drinks. The tea section offers red, white, and green leaf choices, with small and large serving formats. Start by asking the team to explain the available tea options and what the menu labels refer to.

Those labels are not a shortcut to promising a particular personal experience. They identify options on the menu; they do not tell the whole story about an individual drink. Ask about ingredients, preparation, and the available format instead of relying on something you heard secondhand about a color name.

## A crafted kratom drink is another presentation

The crafted kratom section includes named drinks such as Mango Chili Lime, Passionfruit Mint, and Blackberry Dragonfruit Fizz. The menu describes their flavor combinations. A crafted format gives you a different way to choose a drink while keeping its kratom base clear.

Read the complete description before choosing. A fruit-forward name does not mean a drink has no botanicals. Likewise, the separate fruit-drink section is useful when you want a drink with no kava and no kratom. The [current menu](https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu) helps you compare the categories without collapsing them into one long list of colorful names.

## Ask about the item, not just the category

Three questions make a menu conversation easier: “What is the base?” “What else is in this drink?” and “Which size or format does this item come in?” If you need an ingredient excluded or have a food allergy, ask the team to confirm the specific recipe before ordering.

Do not assume that brewed tea, a crafted drink, and another listed kratom format are interchangeable. A similar price or a smaller cup does not make two items the same. Let the team explain the exact item you are looking at. You are choosing a drink, not completing a speed round where asking a follow-up costs points.
