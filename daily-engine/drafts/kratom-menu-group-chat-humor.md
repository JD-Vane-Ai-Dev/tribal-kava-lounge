# The friend who asks what is in everybody’s drink

You know the friend: one round arrives and they hold up each cup like it’s a tiny crime scene. If you’re that friend, you have permission to stay curious — but a few quick menu facts and one-liners will keep the group chat from collapsing into a democratic referendum every time someone orders kratom.

## The one-line menu translation

If someone points at a kratom cup and asks “What is that?” try this short answer: it’s either brewed kratom tea or a crafted kratom drink made from kratom leaf (red, green, or white) served hot, iced, or blended, and the menu will list flavor add-ins and optional boosts. For exact categories and names, check the official drink list: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu (the kratom section lists brewed tea, crafted kratom drinks, punches, shots and extracts).

Why this matters: kratom items are adult-only on the menu, so it’s courteous to remind anyone ordering that kratom products are 21+ with valid ID. See the age requirement and ID note on the menu: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.

## Quick scripts for the friend who asks everything

These one-liners move the order along without shutting down curiosity:

- “That’s kratom — brewed tea or a crafted mix. Want me to ask which leaf they used?”
- “If it looks fruity, it’s probably a crafted kratom drink; if it’s plain, it’s likely brewed tea.”
- “Everything here is non-alcoholic; kratom items are 21+ — do you have your ID?”

The counter staff exists for exactly these questions. The lounge encourages visitors to ask the team about ingredients and flavor paths, especially on a first visit: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/new-here.

## What to ask the counter team (and why)

If the curiosity continues, ask one of these concise, useful questions at the counter:

- “Is that brewed tea or a crafted kratom drink today?” — formats use different sizes and flavor expectations (small/large for brewed tea; set flavor builds for crafted drinks). See beverage formats on the menu: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.
- “Which leaf are you using right now: red, green, or white?” — the menu lists those three leaf choices for brewed kratom tea: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.
- “Does that have any boosts or extracts?” — extracts and boosts are listed as add-ons and require staff confirmation and ID checks: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.

Short explanation for the group: brewed tea appears as small or large on the menu; crafted kratom drinks are named items with specific flavor builds — that’s why a mango-crafted drink won’t taste like the straight tea cup beside it: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.

## Gentle etiquette for the group-chat ordering phase

- Pick a time in the chat so the discussion doesn’t fragment into “still deciding” threads.
- If one friend can’t decide, nominate a taster: pick one brewed tea and one crafted drink to split.
- Remember IDs: the menu requires 21+ verification for kratom items in-lounge, so flag that before you finish the order: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.

A little structure turns the menu rabbit hole into ordering efficiency—and then you can get to the actual point of the night.

## Two tiny truths to end on

1) You don’t need to memorize the menu. The lounge expects questions; tell the team whether you like earthy, creamy, or fruity flavors and they’ll help you pick: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/new-here.

2) Asking is polite. It keeps the group safe and makes ordering faster. If your friend persists, encourage them to ask the kavatender directly — that’s literally the point of the counter team.
