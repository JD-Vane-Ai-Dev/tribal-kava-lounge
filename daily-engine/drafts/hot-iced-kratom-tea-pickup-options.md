# Hot or iced kratom tea: reading Tribal's pickup options

Start here: Tribal lists Hot Tea and Iced Tea as separate pickup items, and the order form walks you through size, leaf color (red/green/white), one optional flavor, and an optional boost. This guide explains what each field on the pickup screen means, what’s required vs optional, and how to avoid surprises at checkout.

## Where the Hot and Iced entries live (and why they’re separate)

On Tribal’s pickup menu you’ll find two distinct items: Botanical tea · kratom · Iced Tea and Botanical tea · kratom · Hot Tea. The hot and iced versions are treated as separate products because they appear with different pickup pricing and slightly different size listings on the marketplace (check the in-lounge menu for in-person prices). Link the menu while you order so you’re looking at the right storefront: https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu.

Fact: the DoorDash pickup listing shows a Small and Large size for both Iced Tea and Hot Tea, and the hot entry explicitly says it uses the same leaf, flavor, and boost choices as the iced version (menu). That means the difference in the form is mostly temperature and how the kitchen prepares your cup, not an extra set of options.

## How to read the size and leaf choices

- Size: The pickup items list two sizes (Small, Large). The DoorDash copy records slightly different price points for Small vs Large; confirm the total at checkout since marketplace prices and in-lounge prices can differ (menu).
- Leaf choice: Both Hot and Iced Tea ask you to choose red, green, or white leaf. This is a menu selection for what botanical leaf is brewed into your tea; it’s a required selection on the order form.

Practical tip: if you’re uncertain which leaf to pick, ask the team through the pickup notes or call the lounge. The menu notes the leaf choice separately for each tea item: "Choose red, green, or white leaf" (menu).

## Flavor, boosts, and what's optional

- Flavor: The pickup form allows one optional flavor modifier from the live flavor list (for example, Strawberry, Passion Mango, Tiger’s Blood, etc.). You do not have to pick a flavor; leaving it blank is acceptable.
- Boosts: The pickup item lists optional boosts with specific size increments (for example, Boost, 5 ml, 10 ml). Boosts are add-ons that appear on the marketplace as adult-use modifiers and will increase the order total. The hot listing says the same boost choices are available as for iced tea.

Advice vs fact: It’s a fact that the online form shows a flavor slot and a boost slot. Whether you should add a boost is a personal choice; if you have ingredient questions or want to confirm what a boost contains, refer those questions to the Tribal team before ordering (new-here).
