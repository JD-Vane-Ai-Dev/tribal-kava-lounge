# Ordering kava for DoorDash pickup: syrup and flavor choices

Start here: if you’re ordering a Single or Double Shell for DoorDash pickup, you must choose exactly one shell syrup (which can be “None”) and you may also pick up to one optional flavor. That required-versus-optional difference is the core of building a correct online pickup order.

## The required choice: shell syrup (you must pick one)

When the DoorDash pickup menu lists Single Shell or Double Shell, it includes a required selector labeled Shell syrup. The options are Brown Cinnamon Sugar, French Vanilla, Hazelnut, or None — and DoorDash expects you to choose exactly one before checkout. Because the menu enforces that field, treat syrup as a required step in your order flow rather than an extra add-on. (See the DoorDash pickup record for the exact listed syrup options.) [Menu: pickup details](https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu).

Why “None” is included: the None option lets you keep the shell unflavored if you prefer a traditional, un-sweetened pour. Picking None counts as your required syrup choice so the order can proceed.

## The optional choice: one flavor slot

Separately, the pickup menu shows an optional Flavor choice for shells: you may pick up to one flavor from the live 18-flavor list (for example Horchata, Strawberry, Passion Mango, Tiger’s Blood, etc.). That flavor is optional — you can use the syrup slot only and skip the flavor, or choose one flavor to add. The menu language and the online modifiers both make this a single optional flavor pick per shell order. [DoorDash flavor list and build guidance in the menu record.](https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu)

Important ordering tip: the flavor list is shared across shells, teas, and slushies on the DoorDash menu. If you see a suggested combination in the online menu copy, treat it as an example, not a guarantee of availability at checkout.

## Putting it together: an explicit Single or Double Shell build

- Step 1 — Choose Single Shell or Double Shell on DoorDash. The page shows distinct item prices for pickup.
- Step 2 — For Shell syrup (required), select exactly one: Brown Cinnamon Sugar, French Vanilla, Hazelnut, or None. Choosing None is valid if you don’t want a syrup.
- Step 3 — (Optional) For Flavor, choose up to one flavor from the 18 live flavor options. Or leave it blank for no added flavor.
- Step 4 — Review the DoorDash checkout total and availability before completing the purchase.

Keep the build simple: for example, Single Shell + French Vanilla + Horchata would be a full build. If you want no extra tastes, choose Single Shell + None and leave Flavor blank.

## Prices and availability: menu record vs. final checkout

There are two separate price records you should treat differently: the in-lounge prices printed on the main menu and the DoorDash pickup marketplace prices. The site’s menu page lists in-lounge prices and notes a separate DoorDash section with merchant-set pickup prices (the DoorDash records are dated in the menu). DoorDash can change availability, fees, and final totals, so always confirm the price and item availability in the DoorDash checkout before finalizing your order. The menu itself links to the DoorDash pickup record and explicitly asks you to check today’s availability and final total. [See the site menu for both in-lounge and DoorDash pickup notes.](https://www.thetribalkavalounge.com/menu)

Put simply: the menu page provides the item descriptions and the DoorDash block lists the pickup prices recorded at a point in time — verify the order in DoorDash right before you pay.

## Quick first-order suggestions (advice, not facts)

- If you’re new and uncertain, pick French Vanilla or None for syrup and skip the extra flavor; that keeps the order straightforward. (This is a user suggestion, not a menu rule.)
- If DoorDash shows an “example build,” don’t assume those specific options will be available at checkout — confirm before you place the order.

## Two quick FAQs

Q: Can I skip syrup entirely? A: Yes — choose the None syrup option; it satisfies the required syrup field.

Q: Can I add more than one flavor? A: No — the DoorDash shell build allows up to one flavor from the live 18-flavor list.

Wrap-up: treat the shell syrup as the required pick (None is valid), treat flavor as a single optional add-on, and always verify DoorDash’s checkout for the final availability and price before you pay. Double-check that final total on DoorDash before you tap Complete.
