# Traditional kava shell sizes at Tribal

Start with the short answer: at Tribal Kava Lounge in West Palm Beach a Single Shell is the basic one‑pour serving, a Double is two pours, a Triple is three pours, and the K.O. Shell is the house stronger shell. The live menu also shows the current in‑lounge prices so you can order confidently at the counter (see the menu section below).

## Why this matters before you order

Shell language is the menu shorthand you’ll hear at most kava lounges. Knowing the names and the price points means you can order without a small lecture or an accidental double‑pour. Tribal serves traditional shells cold in a shell-style vessel and keeps a clear size/price list on the menu (menu link in source notes).

## What each shell means at Tribal

- Single Shell: The straightforward first pour and the common starting point for newcomers. In-lounge price: $6. (Source: Tribal menu.)

- Double Shell: A two‑shell pour — essentially twice the single serving. In-lounge price: $9. (Source: Tribal menu.)

- Triple Shell: A three‑shell pour, the largest of the standard three sizes. In-lounge price: $13. (Source: Tribal menu.)

- K.O. Shell: Described on the menu as Tribal’s stronger house shell. It’s listed separately from the single/double/triple progression. In-lounge price: $10. If you want to know which root blend the bar is using that shift, ask your kavatender at the counter. (Source: Tribal menu.)

## What to say when you order (so it comes out right)

A short script keeps things simple:

1. Tell the kavatender the shell size: “Single shell,” “Double shell,” “Triple shell,” or “K.O. shell.”
2. If you order a Single or Double shell: pick the required shell syrup. The menu lists Brown Cinnamon Sugar, French Vanilla, Hazelnut, or None — choose one exactly. (DoorDash build notes repeat this requirement.)
3. Optionally: request one of the available flavors or say “no flavor” to keep it traditional. If you want help, say “help me choose” and the kavatender will point to today’s live flavor list. (Menu: see sources.)

## A couple of practical menu notes

- Tribal separates traditional shells from crafted kava drinks (creamy, dessert-style options), so if you want the traditional format, stick to the Shell section of the menu. (Source: Tribal menu.)
- The menu also calls out $2 Tuesday single-shell windows; check the live menu for the current schedule before you go. (Source: Tribal what-is-kava and menu.)

## If you’re new and want the smallest commitment

Order a Single Shell and ask for a suggested syrup and a light or no added flavor. If the traditional taste isn’t your thing, Tribal’s crafted kava drinks (the dessert-style lineup) are listed separately and are a reliable alternative; the what-is-kava page gives a quick primer on the difference between traditional shells and crafted formats. (Source: what-is-kava.)

## Final tip: check the live menu and ask

Flavors, root blends, and pickup pricing can change. If a specific price, flavor, or the exact “house shell” blend matters to you, confirm at the counter. The in‑lounge menu lists the four shell options and the current in‑lounge prices so you’ll know what to expect when you order. (Source: Tribal menu.)
