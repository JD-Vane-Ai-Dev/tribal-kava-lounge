## 1. Project Overview

**What:** A premium, mobile-first website for **Tribal Kava Lounge** — a kava and kratom beverage lounge at 770 S Military Trail, West Palm Beach, FL 33415.

**Primary goal:** Beat LowkeyKava.com and every local kava competitor on education, menu clarity, first-timer confidence, conversion flow, local SEO, and perceived brand quality — while competing visually with premium cocktail bars and lounges.

**Core insight:** Most potential customers do not know what kava or kratom is. The site must make the unknown feel easy, safe, stylish, and exciting — teaching without feeling medical, sketchy, smoke-shop, or wellness-template.

**Two structural advantages:**
1. A strong **"New Here?"** education hub (the most important page on the site).
2. A sitewide AI concierge: **"Ask the Kava Guide"** — *Your first-sip assistant.*

**The winning experience:** Lowkey has mystery. This site has mystery **plus clarity**. Visitors should leave thinking: *"I understand what this is. I know what to order. This place looks fire. I feel comfortable visiting."*

**Competitive positioning (reference only — do not copy):**
- **Lowkey Kava** — mysterious minimalist vibe; lacks education, menu depth, first-time guidance, SEO. Beatable on all fronts.
- **Kavahana** — strongest completeness benchmark (menu, education, ordering, articles, catering). Match their infrastructure maturity, beat them on first-timer journey + AI guide.
- **Passage Kava Lounge** — premium lounge atmosphere reference.
- **Kava Social Club** — community/events/reviews reference; avoid their claim-heavy copy.
- **Holy Bula** — right idea with direct education tab; avoid their aggressive effect claims. Beat on taste level, safer copy, premium feel.
- **Premium bar sites (Dante, Broken Shaker)** — hospitality layer reference: reservations, gift cards, private events, press, happenings. This is what Phase 2 adds.

## 3. Compliance Framework (Single Source of Truth)

> This section governs ALL site copy, blog posts, AI assistant responses, social content, and Daily. Every content task references this section.

### 3.1 Prohibited claims — never say:
No medical, health, anxiety, pain, sleep, opioid, addiction, cure, treatment, therapy, supplement, productivity, workout, sedative, euphoria, or guaranteed-effect claims. Specifically never:
- Kratom gives energy / relieves pain / helps anxiety / helps opioid withdrawal / is a supplement / is safe for daily use.
- Kava treats anxiety / helps sleep / melts stress / is risk-free.
- Either product cures, treats, or prevents anything.

### 3.2 Approved language pillars:
Flavor · culture · traditional background · botanical beverage experience · social ritual · responsible adult use · first-time guidance · non-alcoholic lounge experience.

> **Note on "education about kava benefits":** all educational content is framed around **culture, origins, preparation methods, tradition, and flavor** — never health benefits. "People report different experiences, and effects can vary" is the ceiling for effect language.

### 3.3 Required responsible-use language (footer, kratom items, education pages, AI):
- "Kratom products are for adults 21+ only. Valid ID required."
- "Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances."
- "Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- "If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kava or kratom."

### 3.4 Florida 21+ statute (kratom):
Florida law makes it unlawful to sell, deliver, barter, furnish, or give kratom products to anyone under 21, and defines "kratom product" broadly — beverages, extracts, powders, capsules, pills, and other edible forms containing *Mitragyna speciosa*. Therefore **every kratom format Tribal Kava carries (tea, refreshers, shots, extracts) carries the 21+ / valid ID language** — no exceptions, including brewed tea.

### 3.5 Extracts & shots copy rule:
Never hype kratom extracts or shots as "strong," "powerful," "potent," "energy," "boost," "pain relief," or anything effect- or intensity-adjacent. Copy stays neutral and format-descriptive ("compact," "adult-use," "ask our team"). Extracts additionally carry: *Ask our team before ordering.*

### 3.6 Compliance API gate:
All compliance-sensitive content — blog/news posts ("Daily"), AI knowledge base updates, canned chatbot responses, and new menu/education copy — passes through a **Compliance API** check before publishing. The API screens against §3.1–3.5; flagged content is blocked pending edits. This runs *in addition to* human approval, not instead of it.

### 3.7 Operational honesty rule:
Where operational details are unknown, use placeholders. **Do not invent facts.**

## 10. Phase 3 — "Daily" Content Engine (Automation Blueprint)

> Blog/news feature is branded **"Daily"** (not "The Daily Kava" — shelved unless revived later). Lives at `/daily`.

**What it does:** Monitors kava/kratom news nationwide and globally, then produces branded "Daily" posts on a schedule — feeding SEO, the newsletter, and social.

**Should this be fully automated? No.** Kratom is a regulated, litigated, claim-sensitive topic. Auto-publishing scraped kratom news is a legal and brand hazard. Automate discovery, filtering, and drafting; **a human approves every post before publish.** That gate is the compliance shield, not a bottleneck.

**Human gates:** (1) Owner approves/edits each draft before publish. (2) Owner reviews the source list quarterly.
**Trigger:** Daily cron (6am ET) for discovery + drafting; publish on approval.
**State:** SQLite or a simple sheet — seen-URL dedupe, post status (drafted / approved / published), run log.
**Throughput:** ~5–20 candidate stories/day in, 1 digest post/day out (or 1 weekly deep post — see cadence note).

### 10.1 Flow
1. **Fetch sources** → RSS/Atom feeds + Google News RSS queries — *deterministic, no AI needed.*
   Source list (legal, feed-based — no ToS-violating scraping):
   - Google News RSS: `kava bar`, `kava lounge`, `kratom regulation`, `kratom FDA`, `kava Fiji OR Vanuatu OR Tonga`, `non-alcoholic bar trend`
   - FDA newsroom feed (kratom advisories/regulatory actions)
   - American Kratom Association news page
   - Pacific outlets covering kava culture (e.g., RNZ Pacific)
   - Florida/local news feeds for state + county kratom ordinances
   - Industry/lifestyle: mocktail & sober-curious trend coverage
2. **Dedupe against state** → skip seen URLs — *deterministic.*
3. **Filter + classify** → LLM call: relevance score, category (culture / regulation / local / trend / recipe-adjacent), compliance risk flag — *LLM.*
4. **Draft post** → LLM call with §3 Compliance Framework injected into the prompt: summarize in own words with attribution + link to source (never reproduce article text — short summaries only), add local tie-in ("what this means for West Palm Beach kava lovers"), internal links to Menu / New Here? / Visit — *LLM.*
5. **Compliance API check (§3.6)** → draft is screened against §3.1–3.5 (claims, 21+ language, extract/shot hype, statute framing). Fail → auto-flag with reasons, back to redraft. Pass → forward — *deterministic gate wrapping an LLM/rules checker.*
6. **Queue for review** → passing drafts land in owner's approval queue (email, Notion, or Framer CMS draft status) — **HUMAN GATE.**
7. **Publish on approval** → push to Framer CMS via API / webhook — *deterministic.*
8. **Fan-out** → newsletter blurb + IG/TikTok caption drafts from the approved post — each also runs the Compliance API + human approval before posting — *LLM.*

### 10.2 Stack recommendation
- **Runtime (primary): headless agent CLI + cron** — fits the existing agent-pipeline infrastructure (orchestrator pattern, plain-file state, goal.md specs). One scheduled run does fetch → filter → draft → queue. Tradeoff accepted: you own hosting/monitoring.
- **Fallback: n8n self-hosted** — visual RSS → LLM → approval-wait → publish flow, git-tracked JSON, built-in wait/approval nodes. Best if you want less code.
- **Avoid:** Zapier/Make at daily volume (per-task cost) and raw HTML scraping (fragile + ToS risk — feeds cover this).

### 10.3 Cadence honesty check
"Daily" may run thin — kava/kratom news is not a daily firehose. Recommended shape: **daily short digest** (2–3 links, 150 words, low effort) + **one weekly deep post** from the §8.5 evergreen templates. The "Daily" name is honest with a daily digest, and the weekly deep post carries the SEO weight. The weekly post is where SEO value actually lives.

### 10.4 Subscriber incentives (the "Daily" list)
- Monthly subscriber goodie: free agua fresca upgrade or BOGO code (rotates)
- VIP early access to Flavor Drop Day + seasonal signatures
- Birthday drink (birthday field already in VIP signup)
- Monthly merch/gift-card raffle for subscribers who reply or review
- Subscriber-only "first sip" coupon for friends they refer

### 10.5 Sharp edges
- **Copyright:** summarize + attribute + link; never republish article text or images.
- **Compliance:** regulation stories must report neutrally — never spin FDA/kratom coverage into safety or benefit claims. The LLM draft prompt embeds §3 verbatim.
- **Dedupe/idempotency:** hash URLs; a re-run must not double-draft.
- **Source rot:** feeds die — monthly source health check in the run log.
- **Beta CLI drift:** pin agent CLI versions; keep the pipeline runtime-agnostic.

### 10.6 First slice (build this before anything else)
One script, manual trigger: pull Google News RSS for `kratom regulation` → dedupe file → one LLM draft with compliance prompt → run it through a v0 Compliance API (a single LLM-checker call against §3 is enough to start) → output Markdown to a `/drafts` folder. Prove draft + checker quality, then add sources, cron, CMS publish, and fan-out.
