# ROOM 770 — independent venue imprint, one-page website & launch system
Date: 2026-11-19
Parent operator: Tribal Kava Lounge, 770 S Military Trail, Unit A1, West Palm Beach, FL 33415.
Website location: https://thetribalkavalounge.com/private-events
Source: owner brief + REA Prompt & Execution Intelligence, Command Center, Design Intelligence, Offer Lab and existing Tribal venue audit.

## Imprint architecture (mandatory)
ROOM 770 has its own identity, voice, social profiles and customer-facing event offer. It is **not** Tribal Kava Lounge's main attraction, a homepage rebrand, a separate operating address or a claimed standalone legal entity. On the parent website it occupies exactly one dedicated landing-page experience, with the existing owner-controlled inquiry and booking pipeline. The Tribal homepage remains a botanical kava lounge. Do not make the site home hero a venue promotion. Do not spin up a separate website service, payment account, calendar or CRM.

## Brand strategy
**Chosen imprint working name:** ROOM 770.
**Tagline:** Your moment deserves its own room.
**Brand idea:** a flexible, characterful private-events room where the occasion takes center stage instead of the venue dictating a theme.
**Audience:** local adults arranging birthday celebrations, milestone parties, baby/bridal showers, graduations, business mixers, small team events, pop-ups and content/community gatherings.
**Reasons to consider:** an existing ~2,400 sq. ft. indoor venue, 2 pool tables, lounge furniture, 2 bathrooms and optional bar service; custom setups possible subject to real space and supplier confirmation.
**No unsupported promises:** no complimentary banquet furniture, full-service wedding packages, alcohol service, guaranteed DJ/caterer, instant availability, fabricated reviews, artificial scarcity, or unconfirmed fire-code occupancy.
**Capacity:** owner-supplied maximum 75 guests; actual approved layout or official occupancy can be lower and must be confirmed before accepting a booking.
**Brand relationship:** "ROOM 770 is the private-event experience operated at Tribal Kava Lounge." Display this in the footer/FAQ, not the hero wordmark.

## Creative system
Selected direction: **Midnight Botanical Editorial** (editorial type, striking photography, deep forest, brass accents). Two considered alternatives: Gallery Neutral (strong for showers but too generic and inconsistent with approved photo), Electric Afterhours (high-energy night events but alienates family/corporate bookings).
Core colors: midnight forest #0b160f, ink pine #14251a, ivory #fff9ed, metallic-gold approximation #edcf92, border green #49674c.
Wordmark: ROOM 770, bold condensed-feeling lettering; editorial italic accent for the human/memorable word in the headline. Motion: subtle hover and focus states with reduced-motion support, no intrusive autoplay.
Imagery: use the **existing owner-selected edited venue image** with clear styled-concept disclosure, plus actual current-room and community photos. No third-party stock photos or invented furnishings.

## Lead-to-booking workflow (reuse what works)
Meta organic posts / local partnerships / GBP-eligible traffic / search -> /private-events with source UTMs -> occasion shortcut -> date, guest count, requested extras -> Netlify tribal-private-rental -> existing Tribal Studio owner tracker -> personalized itemized quote -> owner-approved Buildry/Stripe invoice or payment link -> receipt-backed 50% booking payment -> owner confirms exact date/time and venue layout -> balance on arrival before setup plus damage-deposit terms -> post-event satisfaction/referral request. An inquiry never becomes a booked date just because a form was sent.
Operating target to *measure*, not claim: quote response within one business day, subject to staffing. Never auto-issue deposits, confirm a booking or DM without consent and verified integration.
Measure: eligible page visits, quote-form starts, completed submissions, qualified leads, quotes sent, paid bookings, median response time, event gross margin; segment by source, event type, add-ons, device. Actual baseline and uplift are unknown until first-party data is collected.
