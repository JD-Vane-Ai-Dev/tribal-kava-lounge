Planning details

Common questions, clear answers.

Is the $250 price for the whole event?

It is the introductory venue offer for 3 event hours on select approved off-peak dates. Extra time is $50 for each approved additional 30 minutes. Rented furniture, catering, DJ and other add-ons are quoted separately. Your final quote confirms the total.

Do tables, chairs and decorations come with the venue?

The venue has existing lounge furniture. Additional banquet tables, chairs, linens, cake/gift setup and décor are optional requests, not included in the introductory offer.

Can I bring my own caterer or decorations?

Tell us what you’re planning in the quote form. Outside food, vendors and décor are reviewed for suitability, any required permissions and the approved room layout before confirmation.

Can I confirm a date or guest capacity online?

Not yet. An inquiry does not hold a date. The venue reviews the requested time, event type, guest count and safe layout before confirming any booking. The venue has a maximum occupancy of 75 guests. Event layouts or specific arrangements may accommodate fewer.

Can I see the space before booking?

Yes, you can request a venue walkthrough by calling (561) 355-0561. Times are arranged directly with the venue.
