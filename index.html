Welcome

Kava and kratom are new to a lot of people. We built the menu to make the experience easier with familiar flavors, clear labels, beginner-friendly options, and a team that is happy to help.

Not Alcohol

Is this alcohol?

No. We are not a liquor bar. Our drinks are non-alcoholic, but kava and kratom products should still be treated as responsible-use botanical beverages.

Deep Dive

What is kava?

Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum). Traditional kava has an earthy taste. Our crafted kava drinks are designed to make the experience smoother and more familiar with creamy dessert-style flavors.

⚠️ Safety Note: Kava is not for everyone. Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. Talk to a qualified professional before using kava if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns.
Deep Dive • 21+ Required

What is kratom?

Kratom is a botanical beverage made from kratom leaves (Mitragyna speciosa). We serve it as traditional-style brewed tea, fruit-forward drinks, compact shots, and adult-use extracts. All kratom products are 21+ with valid ID — ask our team before ordering extracts.

⚠️ Safety Note: Kratom is intended for responsible adult use only. Do not use if pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or if you have health concerns without speaking with a qualified professional. Do not mix with alcohol or other substances.

What Is Kava?

A clear, non-medical intro to kava drinks — culture, preparation, flavor, and how we serve them at Tribal Kava Lounge.

The short version

Kava is a traditional plant-based beverage made from the root of the kava plant (Piper methysticum), with deep roots in Pacific Island social culture. It is not alcohol. At Tribal Kava Lounge in West Palm Beach, we serve kava as traditional-style shells and as creamy, dessert-style crafted kava drinks designed for first-timers.

Where kava comes from

Kava has a long history of communal drinking in places like Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga, and other Pacific communities. The plant is grown for its root, which is traditionally prepared into a shared drink for gatherings — not as a cocktail bar ingredient, but as a social ritual.

How kava is prepared

Traditionally, kava root is ground and mixed with water, then strained. The result is an earthy, bitter botanical beverage. Modern lounges often keep the root tradition while offering flavored formats so new guests are not forced to start with the hardest sip first.

What it tastes like

Raw traditional kava is often described as earthy, peppery, or “dirt smoothie” energy. That is normal for the plant — and exactly why we built crafted kava drinks (Banana Pudding, Tres Leches, Blue Razz Coconut, Dubai Chocolate Pistachio, and more). Flavor first. No mystery menu energy.

Kava at Tribal Kava Lounge

  • Kava shells — traditional-style format. $2 Tuesdays: single shells $2 from 2–5 PM.
  • Crafted kava drinks — creamy, dessert-style drinks (typically $10).
  • Non-alcoholic lounge vibe with free Wi‑Fi, free parking, pool tables, and multiple seating sections.

What kava is not

Kava is not liquor. We do not market it as medicine, a treatment, or a guaranteed-effect product. People report different experiences, and experiences can vary. We focus on culture, flavor, hospitality, and responsible adult use.

Responsible use

Do not mix kava with alcohol or other substances. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kava. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

A straight-talk intro to kratom as a botanical beverage — formats, flavor, Florida 21+ rules, and how we serve it in West Palm Beach.

The short version

Kratom beverages are made from the leaves of Mitragyna speciosa, a tropical tree native to Southeast Asia. At Tribal Kava Lounge, we serve kratom as brewed tea (red, white, and green leaf styles), fruit-forward drinks, shots, and adult-use extracts. All kratom products are for adults 21+ with valid ID.

How we serve kratom

  • Kratom tea — red, white, or green leaf. Small $6.50; large 16 oz $8.75.
  • Kratom drinks — iced, fruit-forward formats like Mango Chili Lime and Passionfruit Mint (~$14).
  • Kratom shots — compact format (~$6).
  • Kratom extracts — adult-use extract options (~$15–25). Ask our team before ordering.

Flavor, not hype

We describe kratom by format and flavor — not medical claims, not intensity hype, not “energy/pain/anxiety” marketing. If someone on the internet is pitching kratom like a pharmacy product, that is not our voice. Ours is lounge, menu, and hospitality.

Florida 21+ rule (plain language)

Florida law restricts kratom products to adults 21 and older. That covers tea, drinks, shots, extracts, and other edible forms. We check ID. No exceptions because “it’s just tea.”

What kratom is not

Kratom is not alcohol. It is not a medicine we prescribe. We do not claim it treats, cures, or prevents anything. Products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. People report different experiences; experiences can vary.

Responsible use

Kratom products are for adults 21+ only. Valid ID required. Do not mix kava or kratom with alcohol or other substances. If you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have health concerns, speak with a qualified professional before using kratom. We do not recommend using kratom as a daily habit or to self-treat any condition.
